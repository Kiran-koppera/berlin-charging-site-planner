      Method and limits

      Data

      Bundesnetzagentur charging register (CC BY 4.0); KBA vehicle stock FZ1 2023–2026; LOR planning areas © Amt für Statistik Berlin-Brandenburg (CC BY 3.0 DE); population from Monitoring Soziale Stadtentwicklung 2025; traffic volumes 2023, Land Berlin (dl-de/zero-2-0); hosts © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

      Model

      Demand per planning area = residents × BEVs per resident × daily kWh × public share (60 % inner city, 35 % outer) plus through traffic. Coverage on a 250 m population grid. Sites scored on gap, traffic, whitespace and host fit. Forecast: stock-and-flow model with quarter-year steps. Validation: out-of-time backtest and a logistic-regression competitor-entry model.

      Limits

      Only devices that completed register notification are included. Demand counts Berlin-registered cars, so city-wide utilisation is understated (the relative gap is not). No grid-capacity data. Costs are planning assumptions, not quotes. Independent portfolio project; not affiliated with any charging operator.
